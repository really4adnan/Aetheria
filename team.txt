=== Why this trio wins ===
Fire/Water/Grass triangle - every weakness covered:
- Charizard 4x Rock -> Blastoise resists Rock, Venusaur Earth Power KOs Rock
- Blastoise Electric/Grass -> Venusaur resists both, Charizard resists Grass
- Venusaur Fire/Flying/Psychic -> Charizard resists Fire/Grass, Blastoise resists Fire
Sleep (Venusaur) + Yawn (Blastoise) beats sample bots that never handle status.
Priority-safe bulk + Leftovers/Sludge recovery outlasts 100-turn limit on HP% tiebreak.
