A LITTLE BATTLE LORE

Three ways to turn the tide.

◈ MEGA EVOLUTION (Gen 6)

Needs Mega Stone + Key Stone. Once per battle, permanent till faint. Charizard → X (Fire/Dragon, Tough Claws) or Y (Drought sun). Venusaur/Blastoise also Mega. +100 BST.

◈ G-MAX (Gen 8 Galar)

Needs Dynamax Band + Galar power spot. 3 turns giant, HP ×1.5, moves become G-Max moves (G-Max Wildfire / Cannonade / Vine Lash). Charizard, Venusaur, Blastoise all have G-Max forms.

◈ Z-MOVES (Gen 7)

Needs Z-Crystal + pose. Once per battle, any mon. Fire → Inferno Overdrive, Water → Hydro Vortex, Grass → Bloom Doom. Upgrades one move to nuke.
